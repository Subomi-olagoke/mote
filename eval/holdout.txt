Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a small red ball that she loved to play with in the garden. One sunny day, Mia lost her ball behind a big tree. She looked and looked but could not find it. A kind bird saw her crying and flew down to help. The bird found the ball under a bush and rolled it back to Mia. Mia was so happy that she thanked the bird and gave it some seeds. From that day on, the bird and Mia were the best of friends, and they played together every morning.
